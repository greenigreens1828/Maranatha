About Us

A neighbourhood store, run the neighbourhood way

Maranatha Store sits right on 1st Street in Tamil Nagar / Slatter Nagar, Karumandisellipalayam — the kind of shop where the shopkeeper knows your usual order. Rice by the sack, dal and oil by the litre, a cart of fresh vegetables at the door, and a counter stacked floor to ceiling with everything else a kitchen runs out of.

No frills, no fuss — just fair weighing, fresh stock, and a shutter that goes up at 6 in the morning.

  • Daily fresh vegetables & fruits, sourced early morning
  • Full range of rice, dal, oil & grocery staples
  • Snacks, dairy, household & personal care items
  • Trusted local weighing scale, transparent pricing
